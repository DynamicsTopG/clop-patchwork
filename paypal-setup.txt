PAYPAL CHECKOUT SETUP — CLOP Patchwork PDF patterns
====================================================
The site is already wired: it just needs 4 PayPal links pasted in.
Until then, Buy buttons keep falling back to Instagram DM.

----------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — PayPal Business account (free)
----------------------------------------------------
- Go to https://www.paypal.com/es/business and create a Business
  account (or upgrade the personal one). Use the CLOP email.
- Business type: "Individual/Sole trader" is fine to start.
- Link the bank account where you want payouts.
- IMPORTANT (security): in Settings, turn on 2-step verification.

----------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — create one payment link per pattern
----------------------------------------------------
In the PayPal Business dashboard:
  Pay & Get Paid  →  Pay Links and Buttons  →  Create pay link

Create these 4, one at a time (fixed price, EUR):

  1. Patrón · Neceser «Ocas»              8,00 €
  2. Patrón · Cojín «Molinillo»           6,00 €
  3. Patrón · Bolso «Cabaña»             12,00 €
  4. Patrón · Manta «Estrella del Norte» 18,00 €

For each one PayPal gives you a link like:
  https://www.paypal.com/ncp/payment/XXXXXXXXXXXX
Copy all 4 links (note which is which).

----------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — hand the links to Claude
----------------------------------------------------
Paste the 4 links in the chat and say "add the paypal links".
They go into the PAYPAL_LINKS block in index.html; the Buy buttons
switch from Instagram DM to PayPal checkout automatically, and the
note under the patterns updates to "Pago seguro a través de PayPal".

----------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 — delivery of the PDF
----------------------------------------------------
PayPal emails you on every sale (buyer name + email + which link).
Reply to the buyer's email with the PDF attached, within 24h —
exactly what the site promises. Later, if sales pick up, we can
automate delivery, but manual keeps it simple and free.

----------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS IS THE SECURE WAY
----------------------------------------------------
- The buyer pays on paypal.com, never on your site. Card numbers
  never touch your page or your GitHub repo.
- Prices are fixed inside PayPal — nobody can edit the page or the
  link to pay less.
- The site only accepts links matching https://www.paypal.com/...
  so a typo or a tampered link can never hijack the Buy buttons.
- There are no API keys, passwords or secrets in the website code
  (nothing to steal from a public repo).
- PayPal handles buyer/seller protection, refunds and disputes.
